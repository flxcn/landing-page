How to uninstall (Safari, iOS)

  1. Return to your Home Screen and find the Princeton Search Engine Engagement Study app icon.
  2. Touch and hold the app icon until the menu appears.
  3. Tap Remove App, then tap Delete App and confirm with Delete.
  4. Removing the app automatically removes the Safari extension. No further steps are needed.
